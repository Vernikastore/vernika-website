# Reference site findings

Source: https://shashankdev-bnshdmm2.manus.space/

The reference presents Vernika as “Digital Solutions & Marketing Services” and uses the positioning “We transform your ideas into powerful digital solutions.” Its service vocabulary includes App Development, Web Development, ETL & Data Solutions, SEO Optimization, Ads & Marketing, Branding & Design, and Digital Marketing. The reference contact details include info.shashankrajput@gmail.com and +91 6307500844, plus GitHub and LinkedIn destinations.

The current Vernika project already covers digital transformation, automation, CRM, Salesforce tools, business management, application showcase, case studies, projects, insights, contact capture, consultation scheduling, and an owner-only admin console. The refresh should incorporate the reference’s additional service categories and contact details where they fit without overwriting existing user-supplied contact/social requirements.

The reference contains named client testimonials and claims such as “30+ happy clients,” “300% ROI,” and “0 to 1M revenue.” These are not copied into the refreshed project because customer reviews, ratings, testimonials, and outcome claims must not be fabricated or presented as verified without user-provided substantiation. New blogs and case studies will be framed as editorial/illustrative transformation examples unless the user provides approved real-client data.

Reference design observations: light background, emerald/green accent, centered hero, confetti-like decorative motion, rounded cards, service grid, testimonials section, and a simple contact footer. The existing project’s dark editorial-tech visual system is more distinctive; the refresh should borrow the reference’s warmth and service breadth while retaining Vernika’s dark navy, coral, peach, and electric-lilac palette, with richer motion and interactive previews.
